📋 Dataset Methodology

Data sources, included variables, and compilation process

🏦 Harvard DataverseDOI: 10.7910/DVN/X2TQQACC0 1.0
Description: Comprehensive database combining 8 data dimensions for the three main neurodegenerative diseases: Alzheimer's (AD), Parkinson's (PD), and Multiple Sclerosis (MS), with a focus on neuroinflammatory mechanisms. The data were compiled through a systematic search of peer-reviewed literature (PubMed/NCBI), meta-analyses, and reports from international organizations (WHO, Mayo Clinic, MSBase), assisted by Perplexity AI in February 2026.

File

1 CSV file: Neuroinflammation_Neurodegenerative_Disorders_Database.csv (11.6 KB). Tabular format with variables in columns and records by disease/subgroup in rows.

Limitations

Biomarker values represent meta-analysis averages and may show considerable inter-study variability. Epidemiological data are global estimates that do not reflect significant regional variations. Clinical trial data correspond to the situation reported in February 2026.
