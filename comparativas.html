⚖️ Comparative Analysis — AD vs PD vs MS

Multidimensional comparison of the three neurodegenerative diseases

🏦 Harvard DataverseDOI: 10.7910/DVN/X2TQQA
Summary: The three neurodegenerative diseases share the central mechanism of chronic neuroinflammation but differ in their clinical, epidemiological, genetic, and therapeutic profiles. AD leads in prevalence (50M) and economic burden ($517B). PD is the fastest-growing neurological disease. MS stands out for its early onset (32 years) and the greatest therapeutic development. This comparative analysis facilitates the identification of cross-cutting patterns and shared therapeutic targets.
