🧪 Neuroinflammation in Neurodegenerative Disorders

Open science dataset: Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, and Multiple Sclerosis  ·  DOI: 10.7910/DVN/X2TQQA

CC0 1.0 FAIR Compliant
About this dataset: Curated neuroinflammation data across three major neurodegenerative disorders. Every number has a citation. Covers CSF biomarkers, neuroimaging parameters, genetic risk factors, clinical progression scales, approved treatments, epidemiology, and comparative analyses. Published under CC0 1.0.
View on GitHub

Disease focus areas

🧠 Alzheimer's Disease (AD)

Amyloid-β and tau biomarkers, microglial activation, APOE genetics, neuroimaging, and approved treatments including lecanemab and donanemab.

 🤝 Parkinson's Disease (PD)

Alpha-synuclein pathology, dopaminergic neurodegeneration, CSF biomarkers, DaTSCAN neuroimaging, and motor and non-motor progression scales.

 🧬 Multiple Sclerosis (MS)

Demyelination patterns, oligoclonal bands, neurofilament light chain biomarkers, lesion load on MRI, and disease-modifying therapy comparisons.

 🔥 Neuroinflammatory Mechanisms

Shared inflammatory pathways: microglial activation states, astrogliosis, cytokine profiles, blood-brain barrier disruption, and NLRP3 inflammasome activity.
