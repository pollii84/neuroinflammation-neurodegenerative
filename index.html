Neuroinflammation in Neurodegenerative Disorders

Interactive dataset: Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, and Multiple Sclerosis  ·  DOI: 10.7910/DVN/X2TQQA

Explore on GitHub

Disease focus areas

  • Alzheimer's Disease

    Amyloid-β and tau biomarkers, microglial activation, APOE genetics, neuroimaging, and approved treatments including lecanemab and donanemab.

    →
  • Parkinson's Disease

    Alpha-synuclein pathology, dopaminergic neurodegeneration, CSF biomarkers, DaTSCAN neuroimaging, and motor and non-motor progression scales.

    →
  • Multiple Sclerosis

    Demyelination patterns, oligoclonal bands, neurofilament light chain biomarkers, lesion load on MRI, and disease-modifying therapy comparisons.

    →
  • Neuroinflammatory Mechanisms

    Shared inflammatory pathways across diseases: microglial activation states, astrogliosis, cytokine profiles, blood-brain barrier disruption, and NLRP3 inflammasome activity.

    →
