🔬 Clinical Trials in Neuroinflammation 2020-2024

Anti-neuroinflammatory therapies in Alzheimer, Parkinson and Multiple Sclerosis

🏦 Harvard DataverseDOI: 10.7910/DVN/X2TQQA9 active/completed trials
Summary: The 2020-2024 period has seen an unprecedented expansion of clinical trials targeting neuroinflammatory mechanisms. In AD, the most advanced trials target TNF-α (Phase 2, n=420) and activated microglia (Phase 3, n=1680). In PD, anti-α-synuclein (Phase 2, n=316) and LRRK2 (Phase 2, n=248) trials represent the most promising approach. In MS, BTK inhibitors in Phase 3 (n=890) could revolutionize progressive treatment. Total participant numbers exceed 4,500 individuals across active/recent trials.
