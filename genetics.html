🧬 Genetics and Genetic Risk Factors

APOE ε4, LRRK2, GBA, HLA-DRB1 — Odds Ratios and Molecular Mechanisms

🏦 Harvard DataverseDOI: 10.7910/DVN/X2TQQA8 genetic variants
Summary: Genetic predisposition is a crucial factor in all three neurodegenerative diseases analyzed. APOE ε4 confers the greatest known risk for AD (OR=3.7 in heterozygosity, OR=12 in homozygosity), being the most potent risk modifier after age. In PD, LRRK2 G2019S (OR=5.1) and GBA N370S (OR=3.8) are the most prevalent variants. In MS, HLA-DRB1*15:01 (OR=3.1) accounts for part of the autoimmune component. Identification of these variants is revolutionizing early diagnosis and the development of precision therapies.
