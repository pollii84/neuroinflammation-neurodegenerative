💰 Global Economic Burden

Direct and indirect annual costs in USD — Alzheimer, Parkinson, Multiple Sclerosis

🏦 Harvard DataverseDOI: 10.7910/DVN/X2TQQA
Summary: The three neurodegenerative diseases analyzed represent a combined economic burden of $738B USD annually at the global level. Alzheimer has the highest total cost ($517B), driven by high long-term care costs, the productivity loss of informal caregivers, and growing demand for dependency care services. Parkinson ($77B) and Multiple Sclerosis ($144B) also represent significant burdens, particularly in indirect costs related to early work disability.
