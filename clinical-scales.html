📊 Clinical Progression Scales

CDR (Alzheimer) · UPDRS (Parkinson) · EDSS (Multiple Sclerosis) — Annual progression rates

🏦 Harvard DataverseDOI: 10.7910/DVN/X2TQQA
Summary: Standardized clinical scales allow functional progression of neurodegenerative diseases to be quantified. CDR (0-3): Alzheimer progresses 0.8 points/year from diagnosis; UPDRS (0-199): Parkinson advances 3.2 points/year; EDSS (0-10): Multiple Sclerosis 0.5 points/year in relapsing-remitting form. These progression rates are the primary endpoints of the most important clinical trials of the past decade.
