🧬 Multiple Sclerosis (MS): Neuroinflammation and Biomarkers

Molecular biomarkers, neuroimaging, clinical progression and treatments — DOI: 10.7910/DVN/X2TQQA

🏦 Harvard DataverseDOI: 10.7910/DVN/X2TQQACC0 1.0
Summary: Multiple Sclerosis (MS): an autoimmune demyelinating disease mediated by T lymphocytes (Th1/Th17) and autoreactive B cells. IFN-γ in CSF is the most specific biomarker (ratio 24.8x). T2-FLAIR lesions are the diagnostic gold standard. HLA-DRB1*15:01 (OR=3.1) is the primary genetic factor. Female:male ratio of 2.3:1. Natalizumab (68% efficacy) and ocrelizumab (46%) are the most effective treatments.
