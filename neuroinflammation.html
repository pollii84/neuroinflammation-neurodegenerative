🔥 Neuroinflammation Mechanisms

Microglia, astrocytes, cytokines and inflammasomes — Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, Multiple Sclerosis

🏦 Harvard DataverseDOI: 10.7910/DVN/X2TQQA
Summary: Neuroinflammation is a central process in all three neurodegenerative diseases, albeit with distinct mechanisms and cellular actors. In AD: microglia activated by Aβ produce IL-1β, IL-6, and TNF-α, activating the NLRP3 inflammasome and amplifying the amyloid cascade. In PD: α-synuclein activates microglia and astrocytes in the substantia nigra, generating oxidative stress and mitochondrial dysfunction. In MS: CD4+ T lymphocytes (Th1/Th17) cross the blood-brain barrier and attack myelin, recruiting macrophages and activating complement.
